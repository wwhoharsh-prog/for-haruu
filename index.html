Dear beautiful human,

Today is a reminder that the world got a little brighter the day you arrived. I hope this next trip around the sun brings you peace when you need it, courage when things feel uncertain, and people who make you feel completely yourself. I hope you find new things to love, old dreams worth returning to, and little moments that turn into your favorite stories. You don't need to make every day extraordinary.
